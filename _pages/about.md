<p class="research-kicker">Evolution · Biodiversity · Plant biology</p>

I am an **M.S. student in Biology at New York University**, with a B.S. in Biological Sciences from Beijing Forestry University. I am interested in how plants evolve, develop, and respond to their environments, and how this knowledge can inform biodiversity conservation.

My experience combines comparative genomics, population genomics, and RNA-seq analysis. I have studied NLR immune-receptor genes across gymnosperms, explored their expression during pathogen infection, and contributed to population-genomic research on the living fossil *Tetracentron sinense*.

I am currently conducting research at the **New York Botanical Garden**, supervised by **Cecilia Zumajo**, on organ identity and development in the aquatic plant *Podostemum ceratophyllum*. I developed the literature review and computational analysis plan, established a reference transcriptome, and screened developmental regulators to guide further analysis of organ homology. [Explore the project →]({{ "/research/#nybg-podostemum" | relative_url }})

For doctoral study, I hope to connect **evolutionary and ecological questions with genetic and physiological approaches**, building on my experience in plant genomics.
