My field training combines species identification and specimen preparation with ecological surveys, experimental design, and quantitative analysis. These experiences connect my computational work with organisms and their environments.

## Comprehensive Field Internship in Biology

<p class="entry-meta">July 2023 · Team leader for the slope-aspect study</p>

I conducted primary-forest surveys and collected plant, insect, and fungal data, including **31 fungal specimens**. I used GPS and measuring tapes to document forest community structure.

I led a **ten-member team** comparing plant communities on north-facing and south-facing slopes. We assessed species diversity using Shannon–Wiener and Simpson indices. I coordinated data organization and visualization in Excel and R and presented the study's findings.

## Jiufeng Forest Reserve Medicinal Plant Survey

<p class="entry-meta">August 2021 · Team member</p>

I helped design and conduct transect surveys of medicinal plants in Jiufeng National Forest Park, with detailed documentation of ten focal medicinal species.

My contributions included collecting and analyzing information for a team dataset covering **547 vascular plant species in 352 genera and 112 families**, recording distribution and medicinal uses, and helping compile the *Medicinal Plant Catalogue of Jiufeng*. The work supported the documentation of local plant diversity and resources for conservation and sustainable use.

## Field Internship in Zoology

<p class="entry-meta">May 2021</p>

I observed birds, insects, and amphibians in the field, studying their identification, ecology, and behavior. Using insect nets and taxonomic keys, I collected and identified **126 insect species** and prepared specimens for the university museum.

I documented bird feeding and nesting behavior and photographed **39 bird species**, sharing the images with local reserve authorities. I also designed and conducted a salinity-stress experiment on frog larvae, estimating LC50 values and comparing survival across salt concentrations.

## Field Internship in Botany

<p class="entry-meta">April 2021</p>

I participated in surveys in suburban forests and the Beijing Botanical Garden, focusing on plant classification, morphology, habitat, and ecological relationships.

I prepared **132 plant specimens** and compiled a plant identification guide. This work developed my practical experience in plant identification, herbarium preparation, and documenting the relationship between plant form and habitat.
