## Meet Chuyi (初一)

Chuyi is my Call duck and unofficial research companion. I raised him from a fertilized egg: after 28 days of incubation, he hatched on the first day of the Lunar New Year in 2023. His name, <span lang="zh" class="unbroken-name">初一</span>, means the first day of the lunar month.

Watching him emerge from an egg and grow into the companion he is today fills me with wonder at the power and resilience of life. He is a much-loved part of my everyday life.
